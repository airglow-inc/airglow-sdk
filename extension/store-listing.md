## Description

Airglow connects your coding agent directly to your browser. Once installed, you simply ask Claude, Codex, or any coding agent to edit a website — it will know how to do it.

Add custom features on top of YouTube, Gmail, LinkedIn. Fix bugs their devs didn't. Eliminate friction in your workflows.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Install the extension. Airglow installs agent instructions to your PC (~/.airglow/AGENTS.md).
2. Ask your coding agent to edit a website: "Make an app to hide YouTube Shorts."
3. Done. The change is saved as an app and persists on page reload.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Add new features — embed x.com and GitHub accounts on LinkedIn profiles to cross-check information
• Create shortcuts for repetitive tasks — generate automated email responses
• Connect your data sources — launch Google Ads campaigns straight from a spreadsheet, in one click
• Keep your changes — everything you build is saved as an app and survives page reloads

COMMON USE CASES

• Marketing — launch Google Ads campaigns straight from a spreadsheet
• Sales — enrich LinkedIn profiles with external data and your CRM
• HR — filter LinkedIn profiles by your custom criteria
• Finance — turn invoice emails into spreadsheet rows
